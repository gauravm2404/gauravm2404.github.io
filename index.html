Dr. Gaurav Maggu Jaipur, Rajasthan · India

Profile

Clinician first. Academic by training. Builder by necessity.

Psychiatrist and academic with nine-plus years spanning inpatient and outpatient care, undergraduate and postgraduate teaching, and high-impact research. Co-architect of ZenQuip — a digital platform positioned as clinical continuity infrastructure for mental healthcare, connecting clinicians and patients through structured CBT tools, courses and AI-assisted support.

Principal Investigator for the All India LLM / ICD-11 synthetic clinical dialogue fine-tuning group, and mentor on the Georgia Tech Create-X ASD technology programme. Recipient of the Solanki Award for Best Research (2024) from the Industrial Psychiatry Journal for work on AI-based suicide prevention.
